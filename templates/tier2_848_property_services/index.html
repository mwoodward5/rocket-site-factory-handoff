How a project runs

Four steps. No sales theater.

01

Call

Phone or text. Two minutes of triage — what, where, when. Often we already know the house style.

02

Visit

Walk-through, measurements, photos, and an honest read on scope, age, and surprises hiding behind the wall.

03

Quote

Line-by-line written estimate. Materials, labor, timeline, and what is explicitly not included.

04

Finish

One crew start to finish. Daily clean. Punch list reviewed with you before we pull the truck out of the drive.
