// Honest answers

The questions we get
every week.

Plumbing is mostly trust. These are the straight answers we give on the phone before a truck ever rolls.

How do I know if I have a slab leak in my Boerne home?
If you've got a warm spot on the floor, a meter dial that keeps moving overnight, and a recent water-bill spike, it is most likely a slab leak. Other tells include the sound of running water with every fixture closed and unexplained damp baseboards. We confirm with acoustic and pressure tests before any concrete is touched.
What's the average lifespan of a water heater in Hill Country water?
Plan on 8 to 12 years for a tank unit, and 12 to 20 years for a tankless unit that is flushed annually. Our limestone water is hard, which shortens tank life. A whole-home softener typically extends both, sometimes well past those ranges.
Do you handle emergency plumbing calls outside 9 AM in Boerne?
Yes — for active leaks or no-water situations we triage by phone and dispatch as soon as a tech can roll, even before our 9 AM standard open. Call first so we can advise on the safest shut-off in the meantime. We work seven days a week from a Boerne base.
What towns and ZIP codes do you serve around Boerne?
We serve Boerne 78006 and 78015, plus Fair Oaks Ranch, Bulverde, Comfort, Pipe Creek, and the north and west reach of San Antonio. If you're on the edge of Kendall County, call and we'll tell you straight whether it's a fit before we roll a truck.
Why is my water pressure low in an older Boerne home?
Low pressure in an older Boerne home is usually a failing pressure-reducing valve, mineral-scaled galvanized supply lines, or a partially closed shut-off from a prior repair. We diagnose with a gauge at the hose bib before recommending anything bigger than a regulator swap.
Are you licensed and insured plumbers in Texas?
Yes — Hill Country Plumbing is veteran-owned, fully licensed in Texas, and carries general liability and workers-comp coverage. License and insurance details are provided on request before any work begins.
Do you offer fixed-fee pricing for plumbing work?
Yes — for most diagnosable jobs, we quote a flat price in person before tools come out. Hourly time-and-materials is reserved for open-ended jobs where the scope can't be known until we open a wall or pull a fixture.
Tankless or tank water heater — which is right for the Hill Country?
Tankless wins on lifespan and footprint, tank wins on up-front cost and tolerance of hard water without a softener. Tankless must be flushed yearly to last in our water. We size both to your actual peak-minute demand, not just bedroom count.
