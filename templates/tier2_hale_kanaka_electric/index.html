Quick answers

The questions Big Island homeowners actually ask us.

Answer-first. No marketing fluff. If your question isn't here, the full set lives on the FAQ page.

01Do you serve Hilo and Kona?
We serve the east side of the Big Island — Pāhoa, Hilo, Keaau, Volcano, Mountain View, and the Puna District. Kona side is outside our standard service area, though we'll quote project-by-project for the right job.
02What EV chargers do you install?
Tesla Wall Connector, ChargePoint, Emporia, JuiceBox, Wallbox, and Grizzl-E are the brands we install most. We also handle universal NEMA 14-50 outlet installs for renters or anyone who hasn't picked a charger yet.
03Are you licensed and insured?
Yes — licensed Hawaiʻi electrical contractor with current general liability and workers-compensation coverage. We email a Certificate of Insurance to any homeowner, property manager, or HOA on request.
04How long does a 200A panel upgrade take?
One day on the wire side, then five to fifteen business days waiting on HELCO disconnect-reconnect scheduling. Lava-zone, owner-pole, or 400A jobs may add a permit cycle.
05Do you handle solar permits and HELCO interconnection?
Yes — end-to-end. We pull every Hawaiʻi County electrical permit and run HELCO net energy metering, customer grid supply, and smart export paperwork. You sign once.

How a project starts

Estimate → permit → install → walk-through.

Every project — whether it's a kitchen circuit or a 12-kW solar interconnect — moves through the same four-stage process so you always know what's next.

1. Free estimate

Call, text, or use the contact form. We schedule a visit, document existing conditions, and email a written scope with line-item pricing. No surprise fees, no high-pressure script.

2. Permit & plan

For panel, service, solar, generator, and EV installs we pull the Hawaiʻi County permit, coordinate HELCO scheduling, and confirm load calculations against your existing service.

3. Install day

We protect your home, communicate every shut-down window, and use marine-grade hardware on every coastal job. Clean work areas, photographed before-and-afters, labeled panel directory.

4. Walk-through & sign-off

Joint inspection of every device, a photo-documented panel schedule, and a written warranty. We don't leave until you can turn the lights off — and on — yourself.
